# Privacy Policy — Rock Paper Scissor Duel

**Effective: May 23, 2026**

Rock Paper Scissor Duel ("the app") collects no personal data, makes no network
requests, and shows no advertising.

## What stays on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, via Apple's `UserDefaults`
API. None of it is transmitted, synced, or shared:

- Your selected color palette
- Your selected idle-icon variant
- Sounds, haptics, no-ties, hold-to-ready, and reduce-motion preferences

Your win/loss score is held only in memory and resets when you relaunch the
app or tap **Reset scores** in settings.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect any personal information.
- We don't request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos,
  location, motion, calendar, or any other system permission.
- We don't include third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs.
- We don't make any network requests of any kind.
- We don't track you across apps or websites.

The bundled privacy manifest
([`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`](Shoot/Shoot/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy)) declares all of
the above to Apple, with `UserDefaults` access reason code `CA92.1`
(in-app settings persistence).

## Children

The app is rated **4+** and is safe for children of all ages. Because the app
collects no data, it complies with COPPA, GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations
by virtue of not having any data to handle.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts collecting data (it has no plans to),
this policy will be updated and you will be notified inside the app before
that version runs.
